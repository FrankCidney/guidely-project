Guidely Internal Operations & IT Support FAQ

Q: What are the IT Helpdesk operating hours?
A: The IT Helpdesk is available Monday through Friday, from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST. For urgent P1 production incidents outside regular business hours, page the on-call engineer via PagerDuty.

Q: How do I request new software licenses or development tools?
A: Submit a software request ticket through the internal IT Portal (it.guidely.internal). Requests under $100/month require manager sign-off, while requests exceeding $100/month require approval from the VP of Engineering.

Q: How do I reset my corporate account password or unlock my account?
A: You can use the self-service password portal at auth.guidely.internal/reset. Alternatively, send a direct message to @IT-Bot on Slack or contact the IT Helpdesk at extension 4357.

Q: What is the deadline for submitting monthly expense reports?
A: Expense reports and supporting receipts must be submitted by 11:59 PM EST on the 25th of each month to guarantee processing in the current pay cycle.
