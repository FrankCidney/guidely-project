Guidely Inc. Company Policies

1. Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy:
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued monthly. PTO requests exceeding 3 consecutive days require manager approval at least two weeks in advance. Unused PTO up to 5 days can be carried over into the next calendar year.

2. Remote Work & Flexible Hours Policy:
Employees are eligible for hybrid remote work, allowing up to 3 days of work from home per week with approval from their team lead. Core working hours are between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM EST. All remote workers must maintain an active presence on company Slack during business hours.

3. Equipment & Expense Reimbursement Policy:
The company provides a one-time work-from-home setup stipend of $500 for ergonomics and peripheral hardware. Monthly Internet expenses are reimbursed up to $70 upon submitting valid receipts via the internal finance portal by the 25th of each month.

4. Information Security & Data Protection:
All company hardware must have disk encryption enabled and multi-factor authentication (MFA) activated on all corporate accounts. Passwords must be updated every 90 days and must contain a minimum of 12 characters including uppercase letters, numbers, and special symbols.
