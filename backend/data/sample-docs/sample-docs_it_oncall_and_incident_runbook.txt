1. INCIDENT MANAGEMENT OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This runbook defines the operational protocol for identifying, triaging, mitigating, 
and reviewing technical incidents across Nova Digital cloud services, databases, 
APIs, and internal platforms. Our primary objective is maintaining a 99.95% system 
uptime SLA while safeguarding data integrity and customer trust.

Every technical employee participates in a blameless culture focused on rapid system 
restoration and systematic root-cause mitigation.


2. SEVERITY LEVEL DEFINITIONS & TARGET RESPONSE SLAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Incidents are categorized into four severity tiers based on business impact, customer 
reach, and operational degradation:

[P1 - CRITICAL OUTAGE]
• Definition: Complete production downtime, total API outage, critical security breach, 
  or data corruption affecting >25% of active users. Core payment/auth flows down.
• PagerDuty Response Target: First acknowledgment within 5 MINUTES.
• Resolution / Mitigation SLA: < 1 Hour.
• Communication Cadence: Status page and executive briefing every 15 minutes.

[P2 - MAJOR DEGRADATION]
• Definition: High latency (p99 > 3000ms), partial core service failure, background job 
  pipeline stalled, or major feature inaccessible for 5% to 25% of users.
• PagerDuty Response Target: First acknowledgment within 15 MINUTES.
• Resolution / Mitigation SLA: < 4 Hours.
• Communication Cadence: Internal Slack updates every 30 minutes.

[P3 - MINOR SERVICE ISSUE]
• Definition: Non-critical feature broken (e.g., PDF export failure, analytics dashboard lag), 
  workaround available, affecting <5% of active users with no data loss.
• PagerDuty Response Target: Acknowledgment within 60 MINUTES (during business hours).
• Resolution / Mitigation SLA: Within 2 business days or next scheduled sprint release.

[P4 - COSMETIC / LOW PRIORITY]
• Definition: Minor UI visual glitches, spelling errors, non-blocking internal tooling bugs.
• PagerDuty Response Target: No on-call page; triaged via standard Jira backlog.
• Resolution / Mitigation SLA: Next regular sprint cycle.


3. INCIDENT COMMAND ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
During any P1 or P2 incident, the following roles must be explicitly declared in the 
incident Slack room:

1. Incident Commander (IC):
   - Assumed by the Primary On-Call Engineer until explicitly handed off.
   - Holds full operational authority. Directs investigation streams, assigns tasks, 
     and prevents duplicate troubleshooting efforts. Does NOT write code or fix bugs directly.

2. Technical Lead (TL):
   - Senior domain specialist or on-call secondary engineer.
   - Diagnoses root causes, reviews error logs in Datadog/Grafana, executes rollbacks, 
     or tests emergency hotfix pull requests.

3. Communications Lead (Comms):
   - Support Engineering Lead or Product Manager.
   - Updates the public Statuspage (status.novadigital.internal), drafts merchant-facing 
     bulletins, and briefs internal customer support teams.


4. ON-CALL ROTATION, COMPENSATION & ESCALATION LADDERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Rotation Schedule: Rotations run on a weekly cycle beginning Mondays at 09:00 UTC 
  and concluding the following Monday at 09:00 UTC.
• On-Call Stipend: Engineers on standby receive a fixed $350 USD weekly standby stipend. 
  Active off-hours incident response hours are compensated at $75 USD/hour (minimum 
  1-hour block per page).
• Escalation Ladder:
  1. Primary On-Call paged via PagerDuty (Phone Call + Push Notification).
  2. If unacknowledged at 10 minutes: Automated secondary page dispatched to Secondary On-Call.
  3. If unacknowledged at 20 minutes: Automated escalation to Engineering Director & VP Ops.


5. STEP-BY-STEP INCIDENT RESPONSE PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Acknowledge the PagerDuty alert immediately within your mobile app or CLI.
Step 2: Initialize the dedicated Slack channel by executing:
        `/incident declare <title> <severity>` in Slack.
Step 3: Open the persistent Google Meet incident bridge posted automatically by the bot.
Step 4: Assess recent deployments in GitHub and ArgoCD within the last 2 hours. If a 
        recent release correlates with error spikes, execute an immediate rollback:
        `argocd app rollback <service-name> --to-revision <last-known-good-commit>`
Step 5: Inspect Datadog APM traces and query PostgreSQL slow logs for locking bottlenecks.
Step 6: Update internal status in `#incident-active` and customer status on Statuspage.
Step 7: Once telemetry stabilizes for 15 consecutive minutes, mark the incident as 
        RESOLVED in PagerDuty and announce mitigation in Slack.


6. BLAMELESS POST-MORTEM & ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS (RCA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Mandatory Post-Mortem Threshold: Required for ALL P1 incidents and any P2 lasting >2 hours.
• Submission Window: The IC must publish an initial blameless RCA draft within 48 HOURS 
  of incident resolution into the Notion Engineering Knowledge Base.
• Structure:
  - Incident Summary & Total Customer Impact Time (MTTD / MTTR).
  - Chronological Timeline of events and actions.
  - Root Cause Analysis utilizing the "5 Whys" methodology.
  - Corrective Action Items assigned to designated owners with strict Jira sprint tickets.
• Review Session: The blameless post-mortem is presented during the bi-weekly Engineering 
  Retrospective on Thursday at 14:00 EST.
